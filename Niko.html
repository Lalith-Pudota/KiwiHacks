"KiwiHacks is New Zealand's first high school hackathon club, running hackathons for high school students aged 13 to 18. Our events are a chance to meet new people, learn some new skills, and build something you're actually proud of, whether that's an app, a game, a website, a robot, or a chair that runs away from you. It's run by high schoolers, for high schoolers. Every site (yes, even this one), every piece of art, and every event was made by Kiwi high schoolers, because a club for high schoolers should be run by them. No matter your skill level, you're welcome. Just sign up, show up, and give it a go."
